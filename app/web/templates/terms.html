Terms and privacy

Plain-language draft for testing. Not final legal terms.

What you're agreeing to

You'll get texts about your own courses from this number: replies to your messages, a morning summary of what's due, a heads-up before deadlines, alerts when a deadline is added or moved, and any reminders you ask for. You can change or turn off each of these by text. Automatic texts are capped at 6 a day, and alerts and heads-ups wait until morning instead of arriving overnight. Text STOP anytime to stop all texts, or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

What it can see

Only what you connect or send: your Brightspace calendar (deadlines, quizzes, exams), and anything you text it. It never asks for or stores your university password, and it can't submit, post or change anything in Brightspace.

How your data is handled

Your calendar link is stored encrypted. Your data is used only to answer you, is never sold or shared with your school or anyone else, and is deleted when you delete your account. To delete it, text DELETE MY ACCOUNT and reply with the code you get back.

AI

Answers are written by an AI using only your own course data. Deadlines come from your calendar, never from the AI's guesses, but always double-check anything important in Brightspace.
