Connect your school once. Then just text.

This page only handles the connection. Questions, reminders and answers all happen in your texts.

What the assistant can see

  • Deadlines, quizzes and exams from your Brightspace calendar
  • Anything you send it: course outlines, grades, "this will take 3 hours"
  • Brightspace notification emails, only if you choose to forward them

What it never does

  • Ask for or store your university password
  • Submit work, post, or change anything in Brightspace
  • Share your data with your school, profs or anyone else

Which school do you go to?

Ontario schools on Brightspace. Canvas, Moodle and Blackboard schools are coming later.
